# 1. Objective

The Social Function Protocol (SFP) defines every interaction between students, projects, organizations and future institutional accounts inside DigiIndia.

Unlike a normal social network, every interaction should have a professional and educational purpose.

Examples

• Discover Developers

• Build Network

• Collaborate on Projects

• Recruit Team Members

• Mentor Students

• Participate in Innovation

• Research Collaboration

• Open Source Contribution
